A stripe field read out of an iteratively warped coordinate. Each wavelength enters the warp a little further along, which is what pulls the colours apart.

The sweep always runs down the colour wheel from Cool to Warm, the way a real spectrum does — so pick a Cool that sits above Warm (blue → orange, not magenta → amber, which detours through green). Far apart gives a full spectrum with a pale overlap; close together gives a narrow single-hue fringe.

Detail and Roughness shape the bend applied to the arcs — they no longer build the shape itself. Bend runs the dispersion direction from fixed (consistent fringe order) to following the field's gradient (fringe widens where it steepens).

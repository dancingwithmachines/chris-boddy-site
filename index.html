What I do

I help organisations work out what they want to say. Then I help them say it in a way that everyone’s happy with.

Sometimes that means a name. Sometimes it’s a whole brand narrative. Sometimes it’s a carefully worded sentence that everyone says was obvious all along (it wasn’t).

I like it. Words can be fun. I mean not fun fun, but fun enough.

Naming, Narrative & Voice, Campaigns, Events & Exhibitions

I’ve been working with Google since 2012.

I named its Dublin HQ The Foundry. I’ve written things seen by millions on Google’s homepage and helped with countless campaigns and launches. Now I’m working with The AI Exchange – Google DeepMind’s new public space dedicated to exploring AI.

Some years ago, a Google product manager told me I was the best copywriter she’d ever worked with. I probably think about that more often than I should.

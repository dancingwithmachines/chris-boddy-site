What I do

I help organisations work out what they want to say. Then I help them say it in a way that everyone’s happy with.

Sometimes that means a name. Sometimes it’s a whole brand narrative. Sometimes it’s a carefully worded sentence that everyone says was obvious all along (it wasn’t).

I’ve done this for Google, Pentagram, Dyson and Apple.

I like it. Words can be fun. I mean not fun fun, but fun enough.

I’ve been working with Google Creative Lab for over a decade. Projects have ranged from naming The Foundry – Google’s Dublin office – to supporting CRM and events teams on campaigns and experiences around the world.

More recently, I’ve been helping shape the narrative and voice for the AI Exchange. Part of Google DeepMind, this is a new space near London’s King’s Cross where people can explore how AI works, how people are using it, and the questions it raises.

I often work as part of Pentagram’s Brand Narrative team in London, helping clients develop their positioning, narrative, messaging and voice.

Collaborating closely with Partners and designers, my involvement covers pretty much everything from early strategic thinking to finished language – for clients like The Wall Street Journal, Waitrose, LEGOLAND and Guy’s & St Thomas’.

As Head of Copywriting at Dyson, I led a team of writers responsible for the brand’s verbal output around the world.

Together, we helped define and deliver almost every expression of the brand – from product naming, packaging and advertising to websites, retail, films, product launches and events.

I was part of Apple’s in-house creative team, developing copy and content as the App Store was being created and rolled out.

I also worked on promotional communications for Apple Retail – bringing the brand’s character and clarity to different audiences, formats and markets.

I continue to work with several independent studios including Campbell Hay, Toaster and House of Greenland – often becoming an extension of their own creative teams.

Projects typically include early brand thinking, naming and verbal identity, advertising campaigns, websites, films, events and experiences.

I work with organisations and creative teams around the world.
